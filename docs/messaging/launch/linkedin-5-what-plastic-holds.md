An engineer on Eng-Tips once asked whether the flatness he wanted on a plastic plate was "asking too much." The honest answer is that it depends.

Here is what it depends on in our shop.

Acetal (Delrin): ±0.005" standard, ±0.002" on a called-out feature. It's the plastic to pick when a dimension matters.

UHMW and HDPE: ±0.010". On anything long, tell us the temperature it runs at. They move with the weather.

Nylon grows when it takes on moisture, so tell us if it runs wet. PTFE creeps under its own clamping. Both get their tolerance stated on the quote, before you order.

Two things lower the price of almost any plastic part. Put the tight number only on the feature that needs it. And tell us what the part does, because the right material often fixes what a tighter tolerance can't.

If a print asks for something the material won't keep, we'll say so before we cut it.
