You send a photo with a tape measure in it. There's a form for it at straightedgemfg.com/replacement-parts, or call.

I look at it and tell you whether I can make it from that, or what else I need. Where it's worn, I ask what it runs against.

If the fit matters, I can 3D print one first so you can try it on the machine before I cut the real material.

Then I machine it. Guides and wear strips are usually UHMW or acetal.
