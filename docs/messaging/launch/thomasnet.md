## Long: paste this

Straightedge is a plastics machine shop in Wauconda, Lake County, Illinois. We machine parts from acetal (Delrin), UHMW, HDPE, nylon, PEEK, PTFE, polycarbonate, acrylic, PVC, polypropylene, G-10 and phenolic, and from 6061 aluminum and brass when the mating part is metal.

Processes: CNC milling, CNC routing of sheet and plate on a 59" × 96" bed, and FDM and SLA 3D printing for prototypes and fixtures. SLA printers: Formlabs Form 4 and Form 3. SLA resins: Formlabs Grey, White, Clear, Tough 1500, Tough 2000, Rigid 10K, High Temp and ESD. Bead blasting is done here; anodize and powder coat are sent out to a finisher.

Pricing: upload a STEP, STL, IGES or 3MF file at straightedgemfg.com for an immediate price and card checkout up to 100 pieces. Milled parts up to 18" × 9.5" × 12" and routed parts up to 59" × 96" in sheet to 1" thick are priced online. Larger parts and larger quantities are quoted by a person within 24 hours. A replacement part with no drawing is quoted from the worn part, sent to the shop to be measured; the original is returned with the finished parts and the quoted price includes that return shipping.

Lead time: standard 7 to 10 business days; expedited 3 to 5 business days.

Tolerances are quoted by material: ±0.005" standard on acetal, ±0.010" on UHMW and HDPE.

Quality: a first-article inspection report and a signed certificate of conformance are available on any order when the buyer asks for them, as a priced option on the quote. Material traceable to the lot.

Straightedge is engineer-led. Your quote and your parts are handled by the engineer who answers the phone.
