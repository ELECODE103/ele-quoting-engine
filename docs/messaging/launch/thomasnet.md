## Long: paste this

Straightedge is a plastics machine shop in Wauconda, Lake County, Illinois. We machine parts from acetal (Delrin), UHMW, HDPE, nylon, PEEK, PTFE, polycarbonate, acrylic, PVC, polypropylene, G-10 and phenolic, and from 6061 aluminum and brass when the mating part is metal.

Processes: CNC milling, CNC routing of sheet and plate, and FDM and SLA 3D printing for prototypes and fixtures.

Pricing: upload a STEP, STL, IGES or 3MF file at straightedgemfg.com for an immediate price and card checkout up to 100 pieces. Milled parts up to 18" × 9.5" × 12" and routed parts up to 59" × 96" in sheet to 1" thick are priced online. Larger parts, larger quantities and parts without CAD are quoted by a person.

Tolerances are quoted by material. Not ISO 9001 certified. No CMM.

Straightedge is a one-person shop. The person who quotes the job programs it, runs it and answers the phone.
