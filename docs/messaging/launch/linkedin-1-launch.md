We opened a plastics machine shop.

It's called Straightedge. It's in Wauconda, Illinois, and it does one thing: machined plastic parts. Delrin, UHMW, HDPE, nylon, PEEK. Milled from block, or cut from sheet on a 59" × 96" router.

Two things about how it works.

You can see the price without talking to anyone. Upload a STEP file at straightedgemfg.com and the price is on the screen. No account needed to look. Up to 100 pieces you can pay by card. Over 100, we quote it within 24 hours.

When you do call, you reach the engineer running your job. Your quote and your parts are handled by the engineer who answers the phone.

About the name. A straightedge is the reference a machinist checks everything else against. It's also what you call someone who deals square. We want the shop to be both.

If you buy or design plastic parts within a day's drive of Chicago or Milwaukee, we'd like to quote one. If you don't, you probably know someone who does.
