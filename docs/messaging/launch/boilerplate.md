Straightedge is a plastics machine shop in Wauconda, Lake County, Illinois. The shop mills and routes parts from Delrin, UHMW, HDPE, nylon, PEEK and other plastics, and 3D prints prototypes and fixtures. Buyers upload a CAD file at straightedgemfg.com and see a price immediately, with card checkout up to 100 pieces; larger runs and parts without CAD are quoted by a person. Straightedge is a one-person shop: the person who quotes the job programs it, runs it and answers the phone. It quotes tolerances by material, prints the size limits of its quote tool, and is not ISO 9001 certified.
