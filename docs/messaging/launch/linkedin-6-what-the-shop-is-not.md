A month in, here's what Straightedge is not.

It is not ISO 9001 certified. If your supplier approval needs the certificate, I'm not your shop yet.

It is not a big shop. It's me. If your quantity or your date is past what one person can run, the quote will tell you.

It has no CMM. I measure the dimensions you call out, by hand, and I'll tell you before you order if your print needs more than that.

What it is: a shop that does plastic, shows you the price before you call, and tells you what the material will and won't hold.

One ask. If you run a metal shop and plastic jobs come in that you'd rather not take, send them my way. And if someone brings me a part that belongs in your shop, I'll send them to you.
