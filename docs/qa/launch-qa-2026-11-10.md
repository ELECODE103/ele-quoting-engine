# Launch QA: combined build, 2026-11-10

Branch `qa-pass` (from `launch-integration` 67eee6a). Ten fix commits, `npm test` 21/21 suites, `npm run build` 22/22 routes prerendered. Screenshots are in `docs/qa/screens/`.

## Go / no-go

**No-go today. Go once the six blockers below are closed.** None of them is a code defect left in this branch: four are deployment or account settings nobody could verify from a test machine, and two are owner decisions about what the site sells and shows.

The combined build itself holds together. Every public page renders without JavaScript with its own title, description, canonical and JSON-LD, hydrates with no console errors, and has no horizontal overflow at 1360, 1024, 768 or 390. All four buyer journeys complete end to end with payment simulated. Seventeen bugs that only showed up with the streams merged were fixed in this pass.

### Blockers, in order

1. **Persistent storage is not configured in the repo.** The database, uploaded CAD, quote drawings and RFQ attachments all live under `data/` in the app directory. `railway.toml` declares no volume, and a comment in `server/routes/api.js` says the database resets on every deploy. If that is still true, each deploy erases orders, accounts, RFQs and customer files. Mount a volume at `<app>/data` and confirm a test order survives a redeploy.
2. **The admin role goes to whoever registers the `ADMIN_EMAIL` address first.** There is no email verification, and `.env.example` names the address. Register the owner account the moment the first deploy is up, before DNS points at it, and again after any database reset. Blocker 1 makes this recur on every deploy until it is fixed.
3. **Email is off until `EMAIL_API_KEY` and `EMAIL_FROM` are set, and nothing tells the buyer.** With them unset, RFQs reach only the admin queue, firm quotes are not sent, and the order page still says "A confirmation is on its way". The server now warns at startup. Set both, verify the sending domain in Resend, and send one of each email to a real inbox.
4. **Stripe has not been exercised against Stripe.** Card checkout, the hosted page, promo codes, the webhook from Stripe's servers and refunds were all simulated. Before launch: live keys, webhook endpoint `/api/stripe/webhook` with its signing secret, one real card order and one refund. Two money questions also need the owner or the accountant first: every order is charged **$0 sales tax**, and the engine's "shipping estimate" is charged as the final shipping price.
5. **The quote tool sells things the owner has not confirmed.** The marketing pages hide every unconfirmed fact; the tool does not. A buyer can pay today for: the turning operation (`turning`), anodize, powder coat and bead blast (which conflict with `nothing-outsourced`), polished edges (`edge-polish`), print sanding and paint (`print-finishing`), and lead times "Standard 7-10 business days" and "Expedited 3-5 business days" with a ship-by date on the order (`lead-standard`). Confirm each or switch it off in Admin before taking orders.
6. **All 14 shop photos are published with their `photo-*` facts unconfirmed.** `docs/messaging/owner-questions.md` item 1 says any photo of a marketplace job has to come down. Owner sign-off per photo.

## Copy, read cold

**As a purchasing agent.** The site says who makes the part and what happens when it is wrong. It does not say what I need to raise a PO: no lead time on any marketing page, no payment terms, no shipping or tax statement, no minimum order, no hours, no street address. "Central time." stands alone on Home and About because the hours fact is off. About's "Paperwork" section has two bullets, one of them about card checkout. The order paperwork now adds up.

**As a design engineer at a packaging OEM.** The design rules are specific and useful (wall, corner, hole depth, web). There is no tolerance number anywhere, which is where I look first; after fix 12 the tool no longer contradicts the pages on this. "Machines and limits" names no machine on Machining and has two bullets on 3D Printing. Material pages are the strongest content.

**Reads as marketing.** Very little. "A machine shop that deals square" and "No sales call unless you want one" are earned by what follows.

**Inconsistent terms** (listed, not changed):

- Acetal is "Delrin / Acetal (POM)" in the tool, "Acetal / Delrin" on Routing, "Delrin / acetal" on Machining, "Acetal (Delrin)" on Parts. Material FAQs say "choose Nylon 6/6 and cast nylon 6" but the tool's list says "Nylon".
- "Quote tool" on service pages, "quote builder" in material FAQs.
- "Manufacturability check", "DFM check" and "the check" for one thing.
- "I" on Quality, About and Replacement Parts; "we" and "us" on Home, RFQ, Materials and in every email.
- Home cards use "CNC Machining / CNC Routing / 3D Printing"; the footer uses "CNC machining".
- Nylon temperatures say "short term" in one column and "intermittent" in the next.

2. Register `ADMIN_EMAIL` on the live site before DNS goes public. Confirm `/admin` opens for it and for no other account.
3. Read the startup log. It should print no `WARNING:` lines.
4. Stripe: create the webhook for `checkout.session.completed`, set the secret, place one real order, confirm the order turns Paid with no page reload, refund it.
5. Resend: verify the domain. Submit one RFQ and one photo request and confirm four emails arrive (two to the shop, two to the buyer).
6. Send yourself a firm quote from the RFQ queue, open the link on a phone, accept by PO.
7. From an order drawer, open each document PDF once and read it as a customer would.
8. Admin → Finishes, Lead Times and the CNC "Turning" operation: switch off everything not confirmed (Blocker 5).
9. Decide sales tax and shipping (Blocker 4).
10. Photos: confirm or remove each (Blocker 6).
