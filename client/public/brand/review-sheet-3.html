STRAIGHTEDGE — round 3: D and E refined, with a nod to machining and additive

Two candidates for each of the directions the owner chose. Each is one shape, explainable in one sentence; each carries both processes the shop runs. Shown in Ink & Brass, one-color, on dark, at 20 mm, as a favicon, on a shelf beside neutral competitors, on a packing slip, embroidered, and as a 6 mm part stamp. The last section shows the two recommended candidates in Ink & Brass and in Charcoal & Layout Blue so colour can be judged apart from form.

Palettes

1 · Ink & Brass — recommendedDeep ink navy with a warm brass accent on bone paper: the gauge, the dial, the data plate. Reads expensive and settled; no shop in the directory owns it.#101C33 · #A8834E · #F4F1EA
2 · Charcoal & Layout BlueCharcoal with Dykem layout-fluid blue as the single signal. Cooler and more technical — reads "engineering" before "heritage". Safe, credible, slightly common.#25282D · #1F418F · #FFFFFF

D2 · Knife Edge

The name rests on the straightedge: top edge dead flat, far end ground to a point, and the heel’s lower corner broken by a 45° chamfer built from three printed layers. Oswald SemiBold +6 %. Alone, the blade needs its plate (an ink square) to be an icon.

D3 · The Cut

The same blade under a name drawn with straight strokes only — every curve is a 45° machined chamfer (S, R, A, G, D). Machining is in the letters, the printed layers are in the heel, the ground edge is the blade. Same plate rule for icons.

E2 · The Kerf, layered

A square of stock parted by one true cut: printed in layers below the cut, machined solid above. The layers need the square to be at least 17 mm / 64 px; below that the solid square is used (`-mark-small`, and the compact lockup). Archivo Bold +3 %.

E3 · The Kerf, light under the edge

A square of stock parted by one straightedge: the upper half is machined and its lower face is the true edge; the lower half is printed to the slope, so its face is a stair, and the light between them is the kerf. Below 8.5 mm / 32 px it collapses to the solid square.
