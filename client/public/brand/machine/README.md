# Machine files — the Straightedge mark

Generated by `scripts/brand/kit.js` from the same geometry as the logo files (`scripts/brand/geom3.js`).

| file | what it is |
|---|---|
| `straightedge-mark-solid-25mm.dxf`, `-50mm.dxf` | The solid kerf square: two closed polylines (machined half above, the other half below the kerf). |
| `straightedge-mark-stepped-25mm.dxf`, `-50mm.dxf` | The full mark: true edge above, six-step printed stair below. |
| `straightedge-mark-embroidery-50mm.svg` | One-colour stepped mark at 50 mm for a digitiser. |

## DXF

- Format: ASCII DXF R12 (AC1009). Entities are closed `POLYLINE`s on layer `MARK`; no splines, arcs,
  text or blocks. Units are millimetres (`$INSUNITS` 4); if a package asks on import, answer **mm**.
- Origin is the lower-left corner of the square; the square is exactly 25.000 or 50.000 mm.
  Scale uniformly for any other size — never stretch one axis.
- The polylines are the **filled** areas. For engraving, laser marking or a pocket, fill inside
  them. To cut the mark out of sheet as two loose pieces, cut on the lines (the kerf is the gap).
- Every corner is sharp and the stair has inside 90° corners, so a rotating cutter leaves its own
  radius there. That is acceptable on the stair; it is **not** acceptable to round the true edge
  or close the kerf.

| size | kerf (narrowest) | riser | tread | largest cutter that still clears the kerf |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25 mm stepped | 1.63 mm | 1.5 mm | 4.17 mm | 1.5 mm (1/16 in.) — laser or engraver preferred |
| 50 mm stepped | 3.25 mm | 3.0 mm | 8.33 mm | 3.175 mm (1/8 in.) |
| 25 mm solid | 1.63 mm | — | — | 1.5 mm (1/16 in.) |
| 50 mm solid | 3.25 mm | — | — | 3.175 mm (1/8 in.) |

Size rule (from `RATIONALE.md`): the stepped mark needs **8.5 mm / 32 px** or more; below that use
the solid square. The 6 mm part stamp is always the solid square. The stair may collapse at small
sizes; the true edge and the kerf must never collapse.

## Embroidery

- One thread colour. On navy or dark twill, bone / natural thread; on bone or khaki, ink navy
  thread. Brass (old-gold thread) is allowed for the mark on bone or white only.
- **Stepped mark: 50 mm square and up only.** At 50 mm the risers are 3.0 mm and the kerf is
  3.25 mm at the step corners, 6.5 mm between them — which is the floor for a clean edge in fill
  stitch with pull compensation. Do not shrink this file.
- **Under 50 mm: the solid kerf square**, down to about 20 mm (kerf 1.3 mm — ask the digitiser to
  hold the gap open; below that the gap stitches shut).
- Fill (tatami) stitch for both shapes, stitch angle along the kerf (20° above horizontal) so the
  true edge is a clean line; optional 1.5 mm satin border. No outline around the square.
- With the name: use the stacked lockup (`e3-light-stacked-1c.svg`), minimum 60 mm wide so the
  letters are at least 5 mm tall. The MANUFACTURING line drops out below 90 mm wide.
