# Stripe branding sheet — Straightedge

Dashboard → Settings → Business → Branding (and Public details for the descriptor). Everything
here is generated by `scripts/brand/kit.js`.

| Stripe field | Use | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Icon** | `icon-128.png` (or `icon-512.png` for a sharper upload) | Square, ink plate, bone mark. Stripe asks for a square PNG/JPG of at least 128 × 128 px, under 512 kB. |
| **Logo** | `logo.png` (1946 × 240, transparent) | Compact lockup, brass mark and ink name, with its clear space built in. Use it where Stripe sets the logo on white or on the bone accent. `logo-on-dark.png` is the all-bone version for a dark background. |
| **Brand colour** | `#101C33` (Ink) | Buttons and links on Stripe-hosted pages pick this up. |
| **Accent colour** | `#F4F1EA` (Bone) | Stripe uses the accent as a background on emails and hosted pages; ink type and the colour logo sit on it correctly. |

Do **not** enter brass (`#A8834E`) in either field. Brass is for the mark and hairlines only — never
buttons, never text — and Stripe would turn it into both. If Stripe's preview shows the logo on
the brand colour instead of the accent, upload `logo-on-dark.png`.

## Descriptors

| Field | Suggestion | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Statement descriptor | `STRAIGHTEDGE MFG` | 5–22 characters, Latin letters, no `< > \ ' " *` |
| Shortened descriptor (prefix for dynamic suffixes) | `STRTEDGE` | 2–10 characters; only needed if a suffix such as the order number is appended |
| Business name shown to customers | `Straightedge` | — |
| Support email / phone / site | `quotes@straightedgemfg.com` · `224-213-6727` · `https://straightedgemfg.com` | from `shared/brand.json` |

The statement descriptor is what a buyer's card statement shows. `STRAIGHTEDGE MFG` is 16
characters and matches the domain (`straightedgemfg.com`), which is what cuts "I don't recognise this
charge" disputes. The legal entity on the Stripe account is Straightedge Manufacturing LLC.

## Check after upload

- Open a test Checkout session and a test receipt email: the logo should sit on bone or white,
  never on brass, and the pay button should be ink with white type.
- The icon is shown as small as 20 px in places; at that size the stair in the mark closes up.
  That is expected and allowed by the brand rules (the true edge and the kerf must stay open;
  the steps may collapse).
